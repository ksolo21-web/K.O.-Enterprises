# Business operating blueprint (approved direction, not launched)

**Value proposition:** An evidence-centered home-inspection reporting operation using an AI-assisted team of system specialists. The inspector or authorized recorder supplies room/area-labeled photos, videos and equipment rating-plate views. The team produces a professionally designed factual report that describes important home components, not merely observed issues. Qualified human oversight evaluates unresolved observations and releases any client-facing report.

**Customer journey:** request → scope and consent → property/room checklist → photo and/or video capture (attic/basement/crawlspace, when safely accessible) → secure originals + hashes → room/system triage → specialty review → manufacturer's equipment research → coverage and missing-views review → professional narrative and large, original media → independent quality blocks → qualified human sign-off → approved report. Requests for unsafe access or destructive investigation are out of scope.

**Unresolved business decisions requiring owner or professional input:** precise customer type (inspector-facing tool versus full-service inspection operation), pricing, service geography, inspection agreements, liability allocation, insurance, field inspector/reviewer qualifications, source retention, AI provider and budget. Do not mistake internal software progress for paid customer demand or legal permission to offer professional services.

## Documented property descriptions, including when no defect is observed

- Foundation type and material; slab-on-grade, raised floor, basement, pier/beam or other support condition when actually visible; uncertainty for concealed components.
- Floor platform/subfloor (OSB, plywood, plank, concrete or unknown), visible joists/beams/posts, and their observed context.
- Wall and roof framing only to the visibility actually available, including trusses versus rafters only with evidence.
- Roof type, covering, drainage and inspection vantage; grading/drainage and exterior cladding; moisture/ventilation limitations.
- Electrical service/distribution, branch wiring where visible; supply/waste piping, fixtures and water heater.
- Heating/cooling equipment, delivery system, model/serial/manufacturing date with manufacturer references and confidence.
- Insulation, ventilation, windows, doors, interior and life safety observations; document inaccessible areas and not-applicable components.

**Report writing standard:** concise, natural, specific, professional. Do not display AI chat traces, hallucinated diagnosis, repair-versus-replace orders or certainty unsupported by evidence. Use specialist referral language and the original annotated/non-annotated image as appropriate.

## Pilot go/no-go evidence

Use a small set of owner-authorized, de-identified real inspection cases with a qualified reference inspector. Measure: required-description coverage, omission rate, unsupported claim count, correct manufacturer-date handling, time to reviewer approval, reviewer corrections, and privacy safeguards. Do not release a client report or market accuracy until controlled field evidence passes the acceptance gates. No pilot has yet been performed.
