# Legal and Intellectual-Property Checklist

This is an operational issue-spotting checklist, not legal advice. A checked box records review, not legal clearance. Escalate uncertainty that could materially affect a person, contract, license, regulator, or launch.

## Opportunity gate

- [ ] Buyer, user, jurisdiction, and proposed activity are identified.
- [ ] The product does not imply an unformed legal entity, unearned credential, partnership, endorsement, or professional license.
- [ ] Regulated domains (legal, medical, tax, finance, insurance, lending, employment, children, biometrics, communications) are excluded or reviewed by qualified counsel.
- [ ] Claims are specific, supportable, and distinguish estimates from actual results.
- [ ] Distribution does not require spam, fake engagement, deceptive SEO, or terms violations.
- [ ] Competition strategy is based on better value—not collusion, sabotage, trade-secret misuse, false comparison, trademark confusion, or below-cost predation.

## Content, code, and data

- [ ] Every dependency, asset, dataset, model, font, image, and copied excerpt has a known source and compatible license/terms.
- [ ] Attribution, notice, source-offer, share-alike, and redistribution obligations are documented and satisfied.
- [ ] No proprietary code, confidential information, leaked data, restricted database, or access-controlled content is used.
- [ ] Product name, domain, and marketing are checked for likely trademark confusion before publication or purchase.
- [ ] User-generated or third-party content has a takedown and rights-handling plan if the product needs one.
- [ ] Repository licensing is deliberate; a public repository is not automatically open source.

## Contract, consumer, and platform

- [ ] Relevant platform/API terms, acceptable-use rules, rate limits, and commercial-use restrictions are recorded with review dates.
- [ ] Any click-through terms, domain purchase, processor agreement, affiliate program, marketplace listing, or customer contract has CEO approval.
- [ ] Price, renewal, cancellation, refund, warranty, support, tax, accessibility, and consumer-protection obligations are defined before billing.
- [ ] Privacy notice, data map, retention, processors, security controls, and rights workflows match actual behavior.
- [ ] Export controls, sanctions, geographic restrictions, and age requirements are considered where applicable.

## Launch record

Record reviewer, date, evidence links, unresolved questions, jurisdiction assumptions, decision, approval ID, and next revalidation date. Any critical unresolved item blocks launch.
