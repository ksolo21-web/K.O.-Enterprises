    FAQ

    Straight answers.

    Anything else? Email ksolo21@gmail.com and you'll hear back within 1 business day.

    How does offline delivery work?
    After checkout, you receive an email with the OAKX Seller Suite kits as a ZIP within 1 business day. Unzip it and open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
    Why is no store login required?
    OAKX tools work from numbers you type and CSV files you export yourself. All processing happens inside your browser on your own computer, so we never need access to your Shopify, TikTok Shop, Amazon, or Etsy account, and your files are never uploaded to us.
    How do updates work?
    When platforms change fees or return windows, we update the presets and email members the latest kit, at least monthly while your membership is active. Replace your old folder with the new one and you're current.
    What is the refund policy?
    If OAKX isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund, no questions asked. Details are in our Refund Policy.
    How do I cancel?
    Email ksolo21@gmail.com or use the billing portal to cancel, update your card, or view invoices. Cancellation stops future renewals; you keep access to the kits you've already received.
