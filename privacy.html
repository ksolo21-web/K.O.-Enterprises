Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

The short version

  • OAKX tools run locally in your browser. Your CSV files and numbers never leave your computer.
  • We don't connect to your store accounts and don't ask for store logins.
  • We collect only what we need to bill you, deliver your kits, and answer support email. We never sell your data.

Who we are

OAKX is a software brand operated by K.O. Enterprise, based in Michigan, USA ("OAKX", "we", "us"). This policy covers this website and the OAKX Seller Suite software.

Data processed by the software

TrueNet by OAKX and ImportScope by OAKX are offline HTML/JavaScript tools. Product data, prices, costs, and CSV files you open in them are processed only inside your web browser on your own device. They are not transmitted to OAKX or any third party. The live demos on this website work the same way.

Information we collect

  • Purchase information: your name, email address, billing country and postal code, and payment details, which are collected and processed by Stripe. OAKX does not see or store your full card number.
  • Communications: emails you send us, such as Founding Access reservations or support requests.
  • Basic hosting logs: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record standard technical data such as IP address and browser type. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

This website uses no advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

How we use it

  • To process payments, deliver software kits, and send monthly updates you've subscribed to.
  • To answer support requests and handle refunds or cancellations.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

Sharing

We never sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run OAKX, such as Stripe for payments and our email provider for delivery and support, or when required by law.

Retention

We keep purchase records as long as required for tax and accounting purposes, and support email only as long as needed to help you. You can ask us to delete information we aren't legally required to keep.

Your choices

You can request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing ksolo21@gmail.com. You can unsubscribe from update emails by replying to any of them or cancelling your membership.

Children

OAKX is intended for business use by adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify members by email.

Questions?
