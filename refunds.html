14-day refund guarantee

If OAKX Seller Suite isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of your first payment on either the Monthly ($9/month) or Annual ($79/year) plan and we will refund that payment in full. You don't need to give a reason.

Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. If a renewal charge surprises you, contact us within 14 days of that renewal and we will review a refund request in good faith. For annual renewals, we will refund the renewal in full if you ask within 14 days of the renewal date.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through our payment processor, Stripe. Most banks post the refund within 5–10 business days. Because prices are flat all-in prices, tax included where applicable, the refund covers the full amount you paid.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time by emailing ksolo21@gmail.com or using the billing portal to cancel, update your card, or view invoices. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, and you will not be charged again. Software kits you have already received remain yours to use.

Delivery problems

Offline software kits are delivered by email within 1 business day of payment. If you haven't received your kit within 2 business days, contact us and we will resend it or refund you in full, whichever you prefer.

Questions?

Email ksolo21@gmail.com. We reply within 1 business day.
