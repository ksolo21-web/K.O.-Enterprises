### Safety posture

- External cash authority defaults to `$0`.
- Scheduled and external operations are paused by default.
- No customer, deployment, validation, or revenue claim is made.
- Routine work is delegated below the Owner/CEO; only owner-reserved exceptions appear in the executive action queue.
