# Business review: advertising alternatives

Observed 2026-09-06. Independent business lens; review of current checkpoint, product dossier and primary external sources. This is a prioritization decision, not evidence of income.

## Decision

**GO for one bounded, unpublished ad-creative service prototype for independent car detailers; HOLD an ad-funded publisher; pause further ImportScope checkout work during this test.** The service has direct evidence that buyers request the deliverable. Neither route has a verified accessible audience or demonstrated willingness to pay K.O. The next constraint is distribution, not another product feature.

This is a temporary first-payment experiment, not a claim that a service is autonomous. Retain the completed ImportScope assets. No new portfolio, ad purchases, subscriptions or recurring agency commitments.

## Three consequential findings

1. **There is buyer-side evidence for creative production, with weak budgets and real competition.** A car-wash/detailing [job listing](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Car-Wash-and-Auto-Detailing_~022085399235036092438/) requests concepts, visuals and copy; its page shows a $10 fixed budget and one hire. The actual paid amount and satisfaction are unknown. A second [detailer listing](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Social-Media-Content-Creator-for-Car-Detailing_~022090467593016088310/) offers $5–15/hour, shows 15–20 proposals, supplies its own photos/video, and asks for skilled editing beyond AI output. Those are demand signals for a category, not qualified leads or proof of a $49 package. Evidence: first-party marketplace postings, medium confidence; recheck availability before any permitted application, no later than 2026-09-13.

2. **Generic ad graphics have strong free substitutes.** [Canva's ad maker](https://www.canva.com/create/facebook-ads/) supplies free templates and drag-and-drop editing. Its [Meta Design Check](https://www.canva.com/apps/meta-design-check/) is free across plans and checks matters such as safe zones. The paid wedge must be verified business-specific copy, coherent choices, clean exports and saved owner time. Automated template resizing alone is insufficient. Evidence: providers' own current capability claims, high confidence for availability, quality untested; revalidate before release or by 2026-10-06.

3. **An ad-funded site adds an audience prerequisite we have not met.** [Google's eligibility guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en) calls for original, useful content that attracts an audience; the [USD payment threshold](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1709871?hl=en) is $100. A functioning site or estimated advertising earnings would not equal collected cash. No eligible site, traffic or RPM is verified here. Evidence: primary provider rules, high confidence; applicability and owner account unverified; revalidate before applying or by 2026-10-06.

The cost of inaction for each buyer is unquantified. Do not translate a possible bad import or unused appointment into invented financial losses.

## One concrete scope and test

Prototype **“Detailer Promo Pack”**, a $49 one-time price hypothesis: three original static concepts, each exported at 1:1 and 9:16; three corresponding captions; one consolidated factual/layout revision. Use purchaser-owned photos and verified service/offer details. Exclude video, posting, account access, campaign management, lead promises and performance guarantees. No invented before/after results, customer testimonials or service capabilities. The buyer buys production work and retains control of campaign spending.

Spend at most two internal hours preparing one clearly fictional sample, exact intake and scope. Measure a full mock order through export and revision; proposed operating ceiling is 90 minutes per order including communication and QC. This ceiling and price are test assumptions. Fees, refunds, time and taxes must be separated from gross receipts; account connection is not a sale.

The coordinator should next identify one exact authorized zero-spend placement with observable qualified interest. Upwork is evidence, not a presumed free channel: [its rules](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062898-Understanding-and-using-Connects) require variable proposal Connects; free allocations are conditional. No Kaleb account or balance was checked. Do not buy Connects or move marketplace prospects off-platform.

**Continue** only after two unrelated buyers complete paid $49 orders, accept delivered assets within scope, and measured contribution remains positive. **Kill/re-scope** if both deliveries exceed 90 minutes or require ongoing account/campaign work. **Reject this offer** after 20 verifiably qualified offer exposures yield zero orders during a 14-day test with functioning checkout. **Insufficient evidence** if audience, authorization or checkout is absent; do not start the clock or treat missing data as rejection. Missing distribution should stop additional build effort, not generate another venture.

Actuals: verified customers 0; revenue $0; initiated spending $0. No outreach, accounts, payments or launch actions performed in this review.
