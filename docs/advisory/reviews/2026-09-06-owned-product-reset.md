# Reset: own a repeatable product

Observed 2026-09-06 after Kaleb questioned the Detailer Promo Pack and challenged the quality of the idea. Coordinator review using the saved business-advisor criteria; no new independent panel or quota retry.

## Decision

Pause the creative-service experiment. Prefer a narrowly scoped **publisher puzzle engine** for the next feasibility and buyer test: one original daily logic game that a small publisher can install on its existing website, with automatic puzzle selection, consistent branding and a printable/newsletter teaser. License a repeatable software product; avoid bespoke content production for every customer. This is a better structural fit for low owner involvement, not proof of sales, an uncontested market or a faster first payment.

Do not build another broad platform or catalog. Proposed differentiation is one original game, dependable isolation from the host page, automated validation and no paid API calls during play. Those properties must be demonstrated. Do not claim that scheduling, branding, analytics or a free widget alone is novel.

## Competition and acquisition reality

[EV Puzzles](https://wordpress.org/plugins/ev-crosswords/) already offers a free functional tier and a Pro tier with automated publishing and retention features. [Analong](https://wordpress.org/plugins/daily-puzzles-for-analong/) provides free daily logic-game embeds through its external service. This is not an empty market. An older [YMC review](https://wordpress.org/plugins/ymc-crossword/) reports page-layout breakage and hypothetical willingness to pay for reliability; it dates to August 2024, is unverified user testimony, and is not a current prospect or payment commitment.

[WordPress.org](https://developer.wordpress.org/plugins/wordpress-org/) provides free plugin hosting, statistics and support forums. [Submission](https://wordpress.org/plugins/developers/add/) needs an account and manual review; listing is not guaranteed traffic or an approved account. Its [guidelines](https://developer.wordpress.org/plugins/wordpress-org/detailed-plugin-guidelines/) require GPL-compatible directory content and prohibit trialware; plan a genuinely functional free plugin and separate permissible paid offering. No submission, account creation, terms acceptance or promotional message occurred.

## Smallest next test

Cap initial technical feasibility at four internal hours: specify one original logic mechanic, create a deterministic generator/solver, and verify at least 1,000 generated boards for validity and the promised solution behavior. Demonstrate one embed that leaves surrounding page content intact, works by keyboard/touch and requires no paid service or server per play. Avoid scraped puzzle content, branded clones and copied publisher puzzles. If the capped prototype cannot do this, stop or re-scope before building the store or product suite.

The first buyer experiment should test a single installable game with a clear paid edition and limited support scope. Price is not yet selected. Require three unrelated publisher evaluations and at least one paid order delivered within that scope before expansion. Reject or change the offer if 20 verified qualified exposures over 14 days with working purchase/delivery yield no orders. Missing audience, permissions, account or checkout means insufficient evidence; the clock has not started.

Direct advertising on an owned consumer game site is a later option if it earns real traffic and qualifies for monetization. It is not the initial revenue assumption. No new product was built in this review. Actual revenue and initiated external spending remain $0; all current payment, outreach and autonomy controls persist.
