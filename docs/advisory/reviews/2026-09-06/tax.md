**The three consequential findings**

1. **The downloadable utility is likely prewritten computer software for Michigan sales/use tax.** Michigan includes electronically delivered standard software; its remotely accessed software discussion turns on actual delivery and the transaction's substance. Here, the buyer receives executable code for local use, with no custom development or remote service constituting the offer. Calling it a browser tool or digital download does not establish an exemption. This is a high-confidence application of Treasury guidance to the documented package, not a Treasury ruling. For taxable retail delivery/use in Michigan, the general rate is 6%; buyer location, exemption status, and the actual sale still matter. [Michigan RAB 2023-10](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/rep-legal/rab/2023-revenue-administrative-bulletins/revenue-administrative-bulletin-2023-10), [Michigan sales/use taxes](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/business-taxes/sales-use-tax).

2. **Gumroad's resale model can handle buyer indirect tax without creating an automatic Michigan registration requirement for the supplier.** Its current contract §§6 and 10 makes Gumroad the reseller/MoR, assigns relevant indirect-tax administration to it, permits deductions, and expressly leaves direct-tax reporting with the supplier. Michigan says a seller whose sales are all through facilitators with Michigan nexus has no sales-tax filing obligation for those sales. It separately says wholesalers do not receive sales-tax licenses. These are distinct possible legal routes: establish the actual Gumroad resale arrangement and any supporting exemption documentation; do not assume every MoR is merely a payment processor or automatically a statutory marketplace facilitator. Neither an unconditional license requirement nor unconditional exemption is supported for the unconfigured account. [Gumroad terms](https://gumroad.com/terms), [Treasury marketplace FAQ](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/business-taxes/sales-use-tax/information/marketplace-facilitator-seller-faqs), [Treasury license FAQ](https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/business-taxes/sales-use-tax/information/sales-tax-license-faq).

3. **The owner retains income-tax and potentially self-employment/estimated-payment duties; a reporting threshold is not a tax-free allowance.** If the actual supplier is an individual operating this business as a sole proprietor, business income/loss generally goes on Schedule C, with Schedule SE as applicable. Net self-employment earnings of $400 or more trigger a federal return requirement; lower earnings can still be reportable and other filing requirements can apply. Current federal TPSO 1099-K reporting uses **more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions**, while direct payment-card reporting has no such minimum. Forms can also arrive below thresholds. The merchant's actual form and gross/net presentation must be reconciled; do not promise which form this account will receive. [IRS self-employed tax center](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employed-individuals-tax-center), [IRS 1099-K guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/understanding-your-form-1099-k).
