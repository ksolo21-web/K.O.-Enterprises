# Decision: subscription-runway profit mandate

Observed: 2026-09-07. Owner: Kaleb. Recorded in the active owner-directed session.

## Objective

Kaleb directed K.O. Enterprises to produce enough **real, verified net cash profit** within the next 12 months to cover one full year of his current highest-tier individual ChatGPT subscription. Kaleb referred to the plan as "Ultra." Because plan names and prices can change, the controlling amount is the actual subscription invoice total when payment is due. The current planning baseline is **USD 2,400 before applicable tax** based on a USD 200 monthly ChatGPT Pro price.

This is a hard operating target, not a promise of profit, a claim that the agent owns a subscription, or authority to charge Kaleb. Revenue counts only after cash is received and clears. Progress reporting must distinguish gross receipts, refunds, payment fees, direct fulfillment costs, tax reserves, and net distributable cash.

## Capital and authority constraints

The existing zero-new-capital mandate remains in force: K.O. Enterprises may not require Kaleb to fund ads, inventory, software, trading capital, domains, subscriptions, or operating losses. External spending, contracts, public claims, customer outreach, payment-account changes, and live financial activity remain owner-reserved actions. No revenue may be fabricated, projected revenue may not be labeled actual, and unpaid leads or demo balances do not count toward the target.

## Portfolio direction

1. **Primary cash-engine candidate — home-surveillance installation services.** Kaleb performs the physical installations. K.O. Enterprises is to build and operate the supporting intake, quoting, scheduling, work-order, payment, follow-up, warranty, review, and online-inquiry systems. Initial product families may include Ring and Google Nest, subject to current product, power-backup, connectivity, licensing, insurance, privacy, and advertising verification. The preferred commercial design is customer-funded hardware or an upfront equipment deposit so the zero-capital rule is preserved.
2. **OANDA — practice-only research lane.** OANDA is not the subscription-funding thesis. Practice data may be used for read-only research and bounded simulation. No live-trading authority, deposit, order placement, copy trading, leverage use, or performance claim is granted by this decision.
3. **Digital products and media — supporting lane.** Existing zero-cost digital experiments may support lead generation or produce independent revenue, but they do not displace the installation-service thesis without evidence of stronger demand and faster verified cash conversion.

## Initial acceptance metrics

The operating scorecard must track:

- actual annual subscription invoice target and remaining gap;
- cleared gross receipts and cleared net cash profit;
- leads, qualified estimates, booked installations, completed installations, refunds and chargebacks;
- customer-acquisition cost, including a verified USD 0 when only owned/free channels are used;
- hardware pass-through, direct labor/transport costs, payment fees and tax reserves;
- average net contribution per completed job;
- days from inquiry to cleared payment;
- OANDA practice status separately, with no demo result counted as business revenue.

The first commercial gate is not a website launch. It is one legally supportable, accurately priced, customer-ready installation offer with a complete work-order and payment flow, followed by one authentic paid customer. The offer must not claim that every wireless device continues operating during a power outage; outage behavior must be specified by product, local battery capacity, internet availability, cellular backup and any required subscription.
