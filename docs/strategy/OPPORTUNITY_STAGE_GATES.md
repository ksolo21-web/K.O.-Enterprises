# Opportunity Stage Gates

## Principle

An opportunity advances only when evidence, authority, capacity, budget, counter-thesis, and required controls all pass. A numerical score prioritizes work; it never creates authority.

Use the existing statuses `candidate`, `researching`, `validating`, `selected`, `building`, `launched`, `hold`, `rejected`, and `killed`. Until a governed launch workflow exists, `launched` remains unavailable.

## Gate 0 — Intake (`candidate`)

Required:

- specific user and painful job;
- initial buyer/budget-holder hypothesis;
- source of the signal and observation date;
- lawful fit with the company charter;
- no obvious constitutional hard stop;
- named Strategy & Portfolio owner.

Authority: `D1` department lead. Duplicates, generic ideas, prohibited categories, and unsupported claims are rejected or held.

## Gate 1 — Research (`researching`)

Required:

- current need and why-now evidence;
- current workaround and cost of inaction;
- economic commitment or credible budget evidence;
- alternatives, competitor density, market structure, and reason the gap persists;
- reachable permission-based channel;
- evidence source, type, confidence, observation date, expiry, and limitations;
- preliminary legal, platform, privacy, security, support, and capital flags.

Authority: `D1`. Research may continue at `$0`; no external exposure is implied.

## Gate 2 — Validation (`validating`)

Required:

- latest executable score passes all advancement gates with current traceable evidence;
- independent counter-thesis and false-positive analysis;
- narrow falsifiable hypothesis;
- frozen success and kill metrics;
- named target segment and measurable behavior;
- time, cash, compute, and owner-minute ceilings;
- legal/security/privacy/claims review appropriate to the proposed method;
- rollback and stop conditions;
- exact approval packet if exposure, outreach, credentials, spend, or deployment is required.

Authority: `D2` President for an internal experiment. External execution remains `D3` or `D4`.

## Gate 3 — Selection (`selected`)

Required:

- actual validation result and denominator, not impressions;
- thresholds evaluated without retrospective weakening;
- surviving counter-thesis and key uncertainty;
- buyer, outcome, wedge, pricing hypothesis, distribution path, and support model;
- preliminary unit economics and CEO-minute estimate;
- portfolio capacity and opportunity-cost comparison;
- President decision to select, hold, reposition, or kill.

Authority: `D2`. Selection authorizes product definition, not public launch.

## Gate 4 — Build (`building`)

Required:

- smallest complete product brief and acceptance criteria;
- product repository and accountable Product & Technology lead;
- approved internal resource budget and delivery timebox;
- threat model, data classification, dependency/license review, analytics plan, accessibility/reliability criteria, and rollback design;
- customer-support and incident path;
- explicit kill condition for build overrun or invalidated demand.

Authority: `D2` inside the current mandate and budget. Paid services, new credentials, or production infrastructure remain reserved/gated.

## Gate 5 — Launch-ready decision (status remains `building`)

Required:

- functional, security, privacy, legal/IP, claims, accessibility, reliability, and recovery reviews;
- truthful public copy and material limitations;
- production account/environment/destination and least-privilege credential plan;
- live pricing/payment/customer-data terms where applicable;
- monitoring, rate/cost ceilings, idempotency, support, rollback, and incident stop;
- current demand evidence and unit economics;
- exact `D4` first-launch approval or matching `D3` standing policy.

`PAUSE_AUTONOMY` blocks execution regardless of readiness.

## Gate 6 — Launch (`launched`)

Only a governed external executor may set `launched` after receiving and reconciling an external deployment/publication receipt. A database status request, score, draft, passing test, or approval row alone is insufficient.

## Operating decision after launch

Measure qualified traffic, activation, conversion, retention, revenue, refunds, delivery cost, support burden, incidents, and CEO minutes. The President may improve, hold, reposition, or recommend scale/kill inside standing policy. Material pricing, spend, account, contract, data, or irreversible changes use the reserved taxonomy.

## Hold, reject, and kill

- Any owner may place work on `hold` when a required fact, dependency, authority, or control is missing.
- Strategy may reject a candidate before portfolio selection with evidence.
- The President may kill internal work for missed frozen thresholds, unacceptable risk, capacity, or inferior opportunity cost.
- Shutdown affecting customers, contractual obligations, money, or a revenue asset requires the applicable owner-reserved decision and exit plan.

Negative evidence, stopped experiments, and reusable assets remain in the audit and decision record.
