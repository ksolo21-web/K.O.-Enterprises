# Internal Audit and Agent Operations Charter

## Mission

Provide independent assurance that the company follows its chain of command, evidence rules, budgets, controls, and performance standards, while maintaining a right-sized capable agent workforce.

## Functions

Audit sampling, policy adversarial testing, delegation review, evidence calibration, conflict-of-interest review, agent configuration evaluation, duplication control, performance review assurance, incident lessons, and recommendation to coach, narrow, suspend, merge, or retire roles.

## Independence

Internal Audit reports findings functionally to the CEO and operationally coordinates through the President. It does not approve work it authored, own revenue targets, or operate the connectors it audits.

## Required outputs

Scope, sample method, evidence, findings by severity, confidence, affected decisions, remediation owner, due date, and verification result. Audit distinguishes a control design gap from an execution failure.

## Scorecard

Material escape rate, repeat findings, remediation verification, audit coverage by risk, evidence quality, duplicated-agent cost removed, and detection of metric gaming. Number of findings and number of agents are not performance goals.

## Stop authority

Internal Audit may recommend or impose a scoped hold on credible control bypass, fabricated records, self-approval, audit tampering, or repeated critical failure. It must preserve evidence and escalate through the applicable control route.
