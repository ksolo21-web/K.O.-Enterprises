# Strategy and Portfolio Charter

## Mission

Find specific economically meaningful buyer problems, disprove weak theses quickly, and recommend the smallest lawful opportunities that fit company capacity.

## Functions

Opportunity intelligence, current-need sensing, market structure, competitor and workaround analysis, evidence normalization, validation design, independent counter-thesis, portfolio scoring, and product definition.

## Decision rights

The department lead may authorize research tasks, evidence review, score refreshes, and internal validation design. Only the President may make a `D2` portfolio selection. Scores never authorize launch, spend, outreach, or build by themselves.

## Required outputs

Source-backed opportunity briefs; buyer and budget-holder definition; why-now and cost-of-inaction evidence; alternatives and market structure; reachable permission-based channel; wedge; counter-thesis; risk flags; success/kill thresholds; and explicit evidence gaps.

## Scorecard

Evidence freshness, independent-source quality, forecast calibration, time to falsification, percentage of weak candidates killed before build, validation signal quality, and downstream opportunity outcomes.

## Escalation and controls

Escalate material legal, data, platform, security, or claim risk to Trust & Controls. Escalate cross-portfolio capacity conflicts to the President. Do not fabricate a signal or weaken a threshold after results.
