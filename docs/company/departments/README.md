# Department Charters

These charters define durable functions, not a quota of agent personas. The Agent President creates temporary specialist assignments when a concrete backlog justifies them. Every assignment remains subject to the root policies, authority matrix, work order, delegation, budget, controls, and pause state.

The functional charter to executable-department mapping is:

- Executive Office: `executive-office`
- Strategy & Portfolio: `opportunity-intelligence`, `strategy-portfolio`
- Product & Technology: `product-technology`, `quality-reliability`
- Commercial Operations: `marketing`, `sales-partnerships`, `customer-success`
- Finance & Administration: `finance`, `business-operations-analytics`
- Trust & Controls: `legal-risk-security` plus the independent control mandates embedded in Finance and Quality & Reliability
- Internal Audit & Agent Operations: `people-agent-operations` plus the independent-audit role in Executive Office
