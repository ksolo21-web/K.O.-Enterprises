# Trust and Controls Charter

## Mission

Protect legality, truth, customers, privacy, security, intellectual property, financial integrity, and reliable operation independently of revenue pressure.

## Control functions

- **Legal / Compliance / IP:** terms, licenses, ownership, disclosures, category risk, regulated boundaries, and approval needs. It does not impersonate licensed counsel.
- **Security / Privacy:** threat boundaries, secrets, least privilege, data minimization, incident handling, and safe integration design.
- **Finance Control:** budget authority, transaction scope, reconciliation, and financial separation of duties.
- **Quality / Reliability:** acceptance, accessibility, performance, recovery, and release evidence.

## Authority

Each function may inspect in-scope work, require remediation, quarantine an artifact, and record pass, conditional pass, or block. Findings name scope, evidence, severity, conditions, expiry, and reviewer. A pass outside its exact scope has no effect.

The operating chain cannot erase or overrule a block. Revenue priority and deadlines do not lower a control. Remediation followed by fresh review is the normal resolution.

## Scorecard

Critical escapes, finding accuracy, review latency, stale-review rate, remediation recurrence, incident containment quality, least-privilege adherence, and customer harm prevented. Finding volume is not success.

## Escalation

Material conflicts route to the Controls Council. Only a lawful owner-reserved risk or policy decision reaches Kaleb. No control role may authorize illegal or constitutionally prohibited conduct.
