# Finance and Administration Charter

## Mission

Maintain truthful financial and resource records, prevent overspend, expose unit economics, and keep administrative demands away from the CEO until a reserved decision is unavoidable.

## Functions

Budget hierarchy, reservations, cost and revenue reconciliation, refunds-to-review, forecasts, unit economics, resource capacity, taxes-to-review, vendor/account inventory, and CEO financial reporting.

## Decision rights

Finance may reserve and allocate resources only inside an active parent envelope and delegation. It may block insufficient, stale, mismatched, or unverified budget. It cannot create cash authority, move money, open accounts, accept terms, or label an assertion as externally verified without evidence.

## Required outputs

Actual/estimate/forecast separation; source references; currency and period; available, reserved, committed, and settled balances; variance; obligations; unknowns; and recommended action.

## Scorecard

Budget variance, reconciliation completeness, unverified-entry age, duplicate/incorrect entries, cash truth, forecast calibration, unit-economics completeness, and finance-related CEO minutes.

## Control independence

Finance Control may block execution even when Finance Administration proposed the transaction. The proposer cannot serve as its own controller.
