# Commercial Operations Charter

## Mission

Create truthful, permission-based paths from a validated customer need to adoption, value, retention, and support with low owner effort.

## Functions

Positioning, accurate content, owned-channel assets, product-led distribution, qualified inbound support, partnership proposals, sales enablement, onboarding, support design, churn analysis, and feedback synthesis.

## Decision rights

The department lead may prepare internal messaging, channel analyses, drafts, and support systems. External publication, prospect/customer contact, live prices, partnerships, and claims require the applicable `D3` standing policy or `D4` approval.

## Required outputs

Named audience, supportable claims, permission basis, channel constraints, conversion event, measurement plan, opt-out/support path, rollback, and claims evidence.

## Scorecard

Qualified reach, activation, verified conversion, retention, support minutes, refund/churn signals, truthful-claim compliance, customer welfare, and revenue per CEO hour. Impressions and bulk output are diagnostic only.

## Prohibitions

No unsolicited bulk outreach, scraped contact lists, fake engagement, undisclosed endorsements, deceptive scarcity, dark patterns, manipulative billing, or claims unsupported by current evidence.
