# Product and Technology Charter

## Mission

Turn validated needs into the smallest complete, secure, maintainable product or experiment while preserving reversibility and reusable assets.

## Functions

Product management, architecture, engineering, implementation-owned testing, documentation, accessibility, performance, reliability engineering, release preparation, maintenance, and technical debt control. Independent release acceptance remains with the Quality / Reliability control function.

## Decision rights

The department lead chooses internal architecture and implementation inside an accepted product brief, repository scope, and resource ceiling. It may reject an oversized or unsafe build. Deployment and external release remain `D3` or `D4` actions.

## Required outputs

Acceptance criteria, threat and failure considerations, implementation, tests, dependency rationale, operational limits, rollback evidence, known limitations, and handoff documentation.

## Scorecard

Accepted customer outcomes, lead time, escaped defects, rework, reliability, recovery evidence, accessibility, security findings, maintenance burden, and resource cost. Lines of code and feature count are not outcomes.

## Escalation and controls

Escalate requirement conflicts to Strategy/Product leadership, budget conflicts through Finance, and critical security/privacy/reliability findings to Trust & Controls and the President. Never deploy merely because a build passes tests.
