# Escalation Policy

## Objective

Resolve ordinary work at the lowest competent level while preserving a clear path for safety, authority, and strategic exceptions. Escalation is a structured transfer of a decision, not a way to avoid analysis.

## Route

```text
specialist -> task lead -> department lead -> Agent President -> Owner / CEO
```

Independent control concerns route through the relevant control lead. A material disagreement among control functions goes to the Controls Council, then to the CEO only when remediation cannot resolve a genuine owner-reserved policy or risk decision.

## Resolve before escalating

For ordinary blockers, the current owner must first attempt the safe in-scope options: verify facts, reduce scope, select a reversible fallback, reassign work, gather missing evidence, split dependencies, or hold the affected task while other work continues.

Do not escalate to the CEO for code choices, research gaps, routine prioritization, agent reassignment, an unavailable free tool, an unvalidated opportunity, or a normal test failure.

## Escalation packet

Every escalation states:

- exact decision class and required decider;
- confirmed facts, uncertainty, and evidence links;
- the blocked objective and unaffected work still moving;
- options considered, including a no-action option;
- recommended choice and why;
- cash, resource, people, data, legal, security, and reputation exposure;
- deadline, expiry, and consequence of delay;
- reversible fallback, rollback, monitoring, and kill condition;
- safe default if no decision arrives.

Silence means the safe default, normally hold. It never means approve.

## Immediate stop escalation

Any role may stop affected work immediately for suspected unauthorized spend, secret exposure, corrupted state, unlawful conduct, customer harm, material falsehood, security compromise, privacy exposure, audit failure, or control bypass. Preserve evidence, scope the stop, notify the relevant control function and President, and continue unrelated safe work.

## CEO attention standard

The President consolidates owner-reserved items into the CEO interface. Interrupt Kaleb outside the normal report only for a time-critical reserved decision or material incident where delay increases harm. Do not split one decision into repeated questions.
