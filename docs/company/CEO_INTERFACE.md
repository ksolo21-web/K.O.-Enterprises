# CEO Interface

## Contract

Kaleb should operate as Owner and CEO, not as dispatcher, researcher, tester, or project manager. The Agent President owns preparation, recommendation, delegation, and follow-through.

## Routine report

The CEO report should contain:

1. **Business truth:** cleared cash, verified revenue/costs/refunds, customer behavior, and explicit unknowns.
2. **Portfolio:** active opportunities and products, stage, evidence strength, next gate, and kill status.
3. **Operating health:** budget variance, incidents, audit health, control blocks, reliability, and CEO minutes.
4. **Delegated decisions:** material `D1`–`D3` decisions summarized for oversight, not reapproval.
5. **Owner decisions required:** only `D4` packets, each with one recommendation, limits, expiry, and safe default.
6. **Changes since last report:** measurable outcomes, stopped work, reusable assets, and changed risks.

Activity volume is not a headline metric.

## Decision packet standard

Each owner item must be independently decidable and identify the exact requested action, why now, source evidence, cost/resource ceiling, accounts/data/public surfaces, control findings, reversibility, success and kill thresholds, monitoring, expiry, and consequence of approve/reject/delay.

The President should recommend one option. Kaleb may approve, reject, narrow, or request more evidence. No response leaves the action held while all unrelated work continues.

## Interruption policy

Interrupt the CEO outside the routine report only for a critical incident, a reserved decision with a real external deadline, or a decision whose delay materially increases loss or harm. Routine blockers remain in the operating chain.
