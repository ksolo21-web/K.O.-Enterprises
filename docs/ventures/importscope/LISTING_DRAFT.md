## Product description

Prepare a readable before/after record for a Shopify catalog update. Compare a current product export with the CSV you plan to import, then review supported price drops, supplied empty fields, product availability and variant differences. The initial audience hypothesis is freelancers handling repeated catalog updates.

Choose two files, review the findings, and download a before/after report. Everything runs in your browser. The offline kit includes the tool, fictional example CSVs and a short getting-started guide. Your files are not uploaded, and the tool does not connect to or change your store.

Supports comma-separated UTF-8 product CSVs, up to 8 MB and 10,000 data rows per file. Both files need a product handle and title. Current and legacy column names are recognized for the documented fields. Reports export as CSV findings and printable HTML.

ImportScope is an advisory file comparison. It does not verify image availability, apps, metafields, market pricing or every Shopify import rule. A missing variant is a review flag, not proof of deletion. Keep a backup and test a small import. This independent tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by Shopify.
