## 1. Agreed business scope

K.O. Enterprises has two active, separately accounted business lines:

1. **K.O. Market Intelligence** — thoroughly sourced competitor research, commercial-service market intelligence, procurement eligibility/fit screening, and client-specific opportunity/decision dossiers. Focus initial validation on Southeast Michigan commercial-service contractors, beginning with cleaning and facilities services. Expansion beyond that niche requires new evidence.
2. **K.O. Contractor Operations Desk** — administrative invoice preparation/submission tracking, authorized vendor portal paperwork, document status, and weekly customer reporting for smaller commercial contractors. No unlicensed debt collection, legal liens, tax/accounting certification, or authority over client funds.

**Deprioritized:** Trading-card/Card Vault venture; do not redirect work or money to it unless the CEO changes priorities.

These businesses share a **research provenance, professional-writing, independent critic, compliance, and document-release control platform**. They do *not* share confidential client information without permission or automatically combine service contracts.

## 2. Non-negotiable product and governance requirements

- **Evidence-first deep research, never generic AI summaries.** For every material assertion, maintain precise primary/original reference, source type, issuer/publisher, date observed, exact claim coverage, short lawfully stored evidence excerpt/location, alternate explanation, conflicts, confidence, client applicability, and expiration/reverification rule. Never fabricate figures, citations, quotations, methods, clients, revenue, or verification.
- **Separate fact / company self-claim / inference / unverified / scenario / recommendation.** A competitor's marketing copy proves only what that competitor claimed, not performance. Multiple sites repeating the same announcement do not count as independent corroboration.
- **Investigation beyond search snippets.** Review original solicitation packages, all relevant addenda, mandatory pre-bid events, eligibility, insurance, geography, bonding and capacity, deadline time zone, history/context, pricing when legally available, and competing evidence. A useful documented **NO-GO** is as valuable as a qualified GO. Unknown eligibility is **HOLD**, not qualified.
- **Client-specific decision support, not commands.** Explain available evidence, defensible options, risks and caveats with conditional/suggestive language. Known factual prohibitions/deadlines must still be stated clearly; hedging is not a substitute for accuracy or a liability shield. Obtain appropriate professional legal/privacy review of service terms and regulated boundaries.
- **Human-quality client artifacts.** Well-edited executive summary, precise sourcing, auditable appendix, consistent units/dates, coherent design, nonduplicative analysis, correct page numbering, accessible contrast, no clipping/overflow, working relevant links, and a meaningful client-specific conclusion. Page count is not proof of depth.
- **Genuinely separate critic execution.** A distinct reviewer must inspect actual source material, calculations, drafts, all PDF pages, and counterarguments; review identity must eventually be authenticated outside self-reported text. A producer cannot approve itself. All **12 mandatory** checks must pass and score must be **strictly greater than 9.0/10**. Failed or untested mandatory checks block release regardless of average. If only an internal self-review is available, label it internal and do not represent it as independent.
- **Fail-closed release governance.** Content digest/version binding, source refresh, real reviewer, client scope, legal/privacy checks, authentic authorization, correct recipient and secure delivery channel immediately before external release. A changed pack invalidates its review. Maintain correction/reissue trace. Neither GitHub Actions nor a database row can approve publication.
- **GitHub source, not public customer storage.** Commit code, synthetic fixtures, sanitized demonstrations, documentation and review-safe test artifacts. Never commit client invoices, personal information, vendor credentials, private market intelligence, privileged client material or secrets to this public repository.
- **Cost and autonomy restraint.** External cash ceiling remains $0 unless explicitly and authentically authorized. The root `PAUSE_AUTONOMY` marker must remain. No automated outreach, scheduled external actions, live integrations, customer sends, purchases, deploys or paid API calls without separate bounded approval.

**Company-wide requirement:** Apply the [Competitor Strength/Weakness & Differentiation Standard](../../strategy/COMPETITIVE_ADVANTAGE_STANDARD.md) to each active or future venture. Preserve critical-path M1; competitor weaknesses and any purported differentiation remain hypotheses until independently sourced and buyer-validated. Competitive advantage assessments must also use the [v1.1 validation playbook](../../strategy/COMPETITIVE_VALIDATION_PLAYBOOK.md) and [unfilled evidence battlecard](../../strategy/templates/COMPETITIVE_BATTLECARD_TEMPLATE.md). Fair-comparison results, switching friction, cost-to-deliver, and buyer willingness-to-pay remain mandatory evidence; no numerical score overrides release or legal gates.

## 3. Distinctive client value proposition to validate

**Working positioning:** *K.O. helps smaller commercial-service businesses make more defensible decisions with original-source intelligence, quantified suitability checks, competitor-context analysis and documented reasons to pass on bad opportunities.*

The differentiation is a **testable hypothesis**, not an established moat:

1. Source-auditable evidence ledger with change history, original documents, dates and limitations.
2. Bid/opportunity **eligibility and NO-GO screening**, not mere alerts or scraped listings.
3. Counter-thesis review, contradictory-evidence search and transparent unknowns.
4. Client-specific operational and economic fit rather than generic market-wide advice.
5. Independent release critic and professional page-by-page document inspection.
6. Follow-up correction, versioning and update notices where properly authorized.

Commercial validation requires real prospect feedback, willingness to pay, delivery effort, actual outcomes and renewals. Initial proposed report and subscription prices remain unvalidated. Do not claim low competition without comparative evidence or claim customers/revenue before external verification.

## 5. Implementation architecture and boundaries

- **Existing source:** `src/company_os/document_quality.py` and `document_quality_batch.py`; do not remove established governance or replace the company operating system.
- **Existing offline CI:** `.github/workflows/document-quality.yml` plus repository-wide CI. Keep read-only GitHub permissions, pinned actions, explicit pause assertion and synthetic/public fixtures only.
- **Future modules (proposals, not yet built):** `source_acquisition`, `provenance_store`, `claim_reconciliation`, `research_queue_adapter`, `independent_critic_adapter`, `pdf_render_audit`, `secure_artifacts`, `release_controller`, and client-facing line-specific pack builders. Implement small, tested units and do not introduce a large new service simply for architectural neatness.
- **Workers:** GitHub Actions can scale finite concurrent tests and permitted **stateless** jobs within plan quotas. It is **not** an always-on agent host, a durable encrypted database, unlimited research compute, or free AI inference. Persistent queues and private client records need separately approved secure hosting and credentials.
- **Real critic vs deterministic gate:** Current gate evaluates the *completeness and internal consistency of supplied attestation*, not whether a different person truly read an issuer source. The authenticated reviewer and source verification must actually be implemented and evidenced; otherwise block live issuance.

## 6. Release gate: mandatory, non-substitutable checks

For **every** issued client pack, retain a checklist and observed evidence for:

1. `source_accuracy`
2. `claim_completeness`
3. `contradictions`
4. `recency`
5. `client_fit`
6. `compliance`
7. `privacy`
8. `professional_writing`
9. `visual_pages`
10. `links_and_citations`
11. `numbers_and_tables`
12. `scope_and_limits`

All 12 are mandatory. Critic **>9.0/10** is required **in addition** to every check passing. Reviewer must be separate and authenticated for client production, inspect actual rendered pages/source material, and provide findings. Record full reviewer report, corrections, retest, immutable pack digest, valid time window, source refresh timestamp, and a distinct authenticated release approval. Do not average away a critical failure, treat an unexecuted test as passed, or present internal self-review as independent.

## 7. Prospecting and anti-slop commercial rules

- Research one **specific** prospect's documented service footprint and relevant current constraints; verify from publicly accessible original company and issuer pages before personalization.
- Draft succinct, specific correspondence around a legitimate operational question or relevant, fully screened market finding. No fictitious familiarity, unsupported economic figures, fabricated “opportunities,” shotgun emails, or impersonation.
- Customer acquisition remains **draft-only** under `PAUSE_AUTONOMY`. A lawful sender identity, mailing address, compliance/opt-out procedures, privacy safeguards and explicit approval are prerequisites to outbound use.
- Research reports must specify the client's decision context, genuine original-source analysis, relevant disqualifiers, alternative explanations, client-fit caveats, and limits. Avoid padded 15-page reports that recycle background or duplicate content.
- Never present an unverified procurement as “recommended.” If mandatory attendance was missed, classify NO-GO absent independently confirmed issuer exception. If qualifications or amendments have not been inspected, classify HOLD.

## 8. Completion rubric and independent review

- **0/10:** No tested implementation.
- **9.0/10:** Still below the company's strict pass threshold.
- **Above 9.0/10:** Potential milestone acceptance only when **all** mandatory tests and actual evidence are present.
- **10/10 target:** Requirements fully met, reproducible real evidence and criticism, no failed mandatory checks.
- **Independent review:** Use a genuinely separate callable critic/reviewer where available and authorized; if unavailable, mark **Internal review only** and keep external client delivery blocked. Reopen any earlier approval if new defects are discovered.
- **Defects:** Register severity, exact reproducible case and affected content hash; fix, rerun impacted tests and all gating checks; do not carry an earlier approval onto changed content.
